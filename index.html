How to use this tracker

Fill out the form below to track your spending:

  1. Enter the Expense Name (e.g., Coffee, Rent).
  2. Enter the total Amount.
  3. Select a Category from the dropdown.
  4. Choose the Date of purchase.
  5. Click Add Expense to log it into your table.
